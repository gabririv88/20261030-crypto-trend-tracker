How to read this

  • Paper trading only. No orders are placed and no account is connected. Prices are Coinbase daily candles (UTC).
  • Every trade pays 0.14% per side (0.09% Revolut X taker fee plus 0.05% assumed slippage). Each coin starts with $1,000 per model.
  • The replay part of the chart uses today's coin list on past prices, so it is flattered by coins that survived. It shows how the models behave, not what they earned.
  • Fewer than 90 days of live tracking says almost nothing. Judge on drawdown, risk-adjusted return and the gap to buy and hold, after at least a year.
